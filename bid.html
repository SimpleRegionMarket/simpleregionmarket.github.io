Bid-Sign

The Bid-Sign is an Auction for a Region. The highes Price wins. YOu can add you ammount (1$) with hitting the Sign. If you leave Region-Line Empty, SRM will automatic add the Region where teh sign stands on. The Price have to be a Number and the Endtime have to be a Numbic. Means you have to write the Endtime and add a Function like Minutes (m), Hours (h) or Days (d). You can´t create a Sign on a Region or for a Region if you are not Owner or no OP.
