Let -Sign


With the Let-Sign you can rent a Region endless. You will pay every Time the Price. To stop this you have to hit the Sign aigain. If you place it on a Region and leave the Region-Line empty, it will automatic add the Region, where it stands on. The Price have to be a Number and the time have to be a Numbic. Means you have to write the Time and add a Function like Minutes (m), Hours (h) or Days (d). You can´t create a Sign on a Region or for a Region if you are not Owner or no OP.
