Usage

Setting up your SRM signs is very easy. There are some caveats to know before you begin.

  1. You must own the region you are going to put on the market. Check for WG owner status with /rg info <regionID>.
  2. You must have the corresponding SRM permission node to create the specific sign type.
  3. Check the Tutorials for example WorldGuard region setups.
  4. There is no confirmation when clicking on SRM signs! Make sure your players are aware of this.
  5. SRM signs are able to auto-detect the region they are placed in. Adding the region ID is dependent on your use case.
  6. It is good practice and may save you headaches in the long run to use all lower case letters for your region IDs when creating the region with WG and when making the SRM signs.

 

Sell Sign

Selling plots will transfer ownership from one player to another. Setup your [SELL] signs exactly as shown below.

ExampleExplanation
* The first line must say [SELL] to set the region For Sale
* The second line is the WorldGuard region ID for your plot
* The third line is the price you are selling the plot for
 -=-> Do not add the dollar sign, other currency character, or comma
* The forth line is the player name and is optional

 

Rent Sign

Renting plots will add a player as a region member. Players will rent the plot for a fixed price and duration. Players are able to extend their rental period up to a pre-configured amount of time or number of times by clicking on the sign again. Setup your [RENT] signs exactly as shown below.

ExampleExplanation
* The first line must say [RENT] to set the region For Rent
* The second line is the WorldGuard region ID for your plot
* The third line is the price you are renting the plot for and the duration
 -=-> Do not add the dollar sign, other currency character, or comma
-=->The duration can be written as 1m 1h 1d for minutes, hours, days
-=->Do not add a space before or after the colon :
* The forth line is the player name and is optional

 

Let Sign

Leasing plots will add a player as a region member. Players will lease the plot for a fixed price and duration. Leases will automatically renew indefinitely so long as the player has enough money to cover the bill. The lease can be terminated at any time by the player by clicking on the sign again. Setup your [LET] signs exactly as shown below.

ExampleExplanation
* The first line must say [LET] to set the region For Let
* The second line is the WorldGuard region ID for your plot
* The third line is the price you are leasing the plot for and the duration
 -=-> Do not add the dollar sign, other currency character, or comma
-=->The duration can be written as 1m 1h 1d for minutes, hours, days
-=->Do not add a space before or after the colon :
* The forth line is the player name and is optional

 

Bid Sign

Players are able to auction plots they own. An auction will run for a duration of time determined when creating the sign. The starting bid is listed on the sign. Players can place a bid on the plot by clicking on the sign. Each bid is increased by the value of the starting bid. Setup your [BID] signs exactly as shown below.

ExampleExplanation
* The first line must say [BID] to set the region For BID
* The second line is the WorldGuard region ID for your plot
* The third line is the starting price for the auction and how long the auction will be open
 -=-> Do not add the dollar sign, other currency character, or comma
-=->The duration can be written as 1m 1h 1d for minutes, hours, days
-=->Do not add a space before or after the colon :
* The forth line is the player name and is optional
